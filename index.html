QUESTIONS, ANSWERED

FAQ

Can I send my own 3D design?

Yes. You can share compatible design files or references. We'll review the file and confirm printability, size, material and pricing before production.

Can you create a model from a photo?

We can review your photo/reference and tell you what is possible. Some projects may require additional design or modelling work.

Do you ship across India?

Yes. Local delivery and pickup are also available in Bengaluru, subject to the final order and delivery arrangement.

Can I order a functional part or prototype?

Yes. Share the dimensions, reference, existing part or design and intended use. We'll review the requirements and quote accordingly.

How do custom prices work?

Custom pricing depends on design complexity, material, size, print time, quantity, finishing and shipping. We provide a quote before production.
